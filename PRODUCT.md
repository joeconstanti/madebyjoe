## Users

Inferred from the site's content and calls to action: prospective clients, collaborators, and people exploring Joe's work. They need to understand the range of his work, find the right studio or business for a project, and contact him.

## Product Purpose

Inferred from the portfolio: introduce Joe Constanti as a London-based designer and technologist, present selected work and related ventures, and help visitors choose a relevant destination or start a conversation.

## Positioning

Inferred from the site's structure and copy: Joe connects design and digital production with 3D, AI research, private AI hosting, and automation through a group of specialist ventures. The portfolio acts as a single entry point across that work. This is a synthesis of the current site, not a separately confirmed positioning statement.

## Operating Context

Visitors browse a single-page portfolio, explore five linked destinations (From the Root, Synthetic Labs, Cerebral, Automate Digital, and joe.graphics), and can contact Joe by email or through social profiles. The site identifies Joe as based in London and working worldwide.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve existing project destinations, contact channels, supplied analytics configuration, and material site content unless Joe requests a change.
- Do not invent client relationships, results, testimonials, metrics, service capabilities, or other evidence. Verify existing claims before expanding or reusing them elsewhere.
- Product details and audience needs beyond what the current site states remain unconfirmed.

## Brand Commitments

The site uses the name madebyjoe and identifies its subject as Joe Constanti, a designer and technologist. Preserve the supplied business and project names and their destinations.

## Product Principles

- Make Joe's range of work understandable without obscuring the individual ventures.
- Help visitors find the most relevant project destination or contact route.
- Keep portfolio statements grounded in the site's actual work and verifiable evidence.
- Preserve established destinations and measurement configuration during unrelated changes.
