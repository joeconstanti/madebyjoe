About

Hello, I’m Joe. I like to make things: campaigns, 3D worlds, websites, AI tools and the systems that run them.

I started in digital design and development, making campaigns and builds for brands like Samsung, Audi and Absolut. The work grew, so it split into companies: a studio for design and 3D, a lab for AI research, a cloud to host it and an agency to automate it. joe.graphics is where my personal design work lives.

Want me to make something for you? Pick the one that fits, or write to me and I will point you to the right one.
